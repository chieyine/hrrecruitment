# 12. Emergency recruitment

Emergency recruitment is faster. It is not less controlled. The distinction runs through every part of the design: the platform shortens **timelines** and reuses **pre-approved material**, and it never removes an approval, a check or an audit record.

## 12.1 Classification

A vacancy is marked as emergency recruitment with a written justification. The classification is then approved separately from the vacancy content, by an HR Manager who does not own the vacancy. An unapproved classification blocks publication.

An emergency staffing request also escalates automatically to an executive approver.

## 12.2 Shortened service standards

| Stage | Emergency | Standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Staffing request review | 4 hours | 72 hours |
| Budget confirmation | 4 hours | 72 hours |
| Vacancy approval | 4 hours | 72 hours |
| Advertising period | 48 hours | 336 hours |
| Longlisting | 4 hours | 72 hours |
| Shortlisting | 8 hours | 120 hours |
| Assessment | 24 hours | 168 hours |
| Interview | 24 hours | 168 hours |
| Reference checking | 24 hours | 120 hours |
| Offer approval | 4 hours | 72 hours |
| Pre-employment clearance | 48 hours | 240 hours |

These drive due dates and escalation. They do not permit a stage to be skipped — only chased sooner.

## 12.3 The advertising floor

An emergency vacancy must still be open for at least **24 hours**. A vacancy nobody could realistically see was never advertised, and shortening the window to nothing would make the whole exercise a direct appointment wearing a competitive label.

## 12.4 Controls that are never waived

Five controls hold regardless of urgency, and they are evaluated from evidence already in the record — an emergency hire cannot reach offer approval simply by moving faster than the checks.

- Identity verification
- Safeguarding check
- At least one satisfactory reference
- Budget Holder funding confirmation
- HR Manager offer approval

An explicitly waived check counts as settled, because a waiver is itself a recorded HR Manager decision. A check merely *in progress* does not.

## 12.5 Accelerations available

- Pre-approved job descriptions attached to the staffing request
- Pre-approved assessment templates
- Candidates drawn from an existing roster, already assessed and reference-checked
- Rapid availability checks against roster records
- Bulk candidate communication
- Same-day or next-day assessment scheduling
- Remote interviews
- Expedited offer preparation

## 12.6 Post-recruitment compliance review

Every emergency exercise is reviewed afterwards. The review is assembled from what actually happened rather than from a form filled in later, so it cannot be written to look tidier than the record.

It reports the justification, who approved the classification and when, the time from advertising to offer, every acceleration that was used, every non-waivable control that was satisfied, and any findings.

Findings are raised where:

- No written justification was recorded
- The classification was never approved by an authorised officer
- The advertising period fell below the 24-hour minimum
- Any non-waivable control was not completed
- **The exercise was classified as an emergency but used no accelerated provisions at all**

That last one is the most interesting finding the review produces. An emergency classification that changed nothing about how the recruitment ran suggests the classification was unnecessary — and repeated instances suggest it is being used to bypass scrutiny rather than to move quickly.

## 12.7 Bulk recruitment

For high-volume exercises the platform supports bulk application review, bulk status changes with a preview-then-apply flow and an undo window, bulk communication that never exposes one candidate's address to another, bulk interview scheduling, multiple panels, assessment-centre management, candidate grouping, attendance lists, batch score upload, batch document and reference requests, and batch ERP transfer after final approval.

Every bulk action previews what will change, reports what is ineligible and why, and leaves ineligible records untouched.

## 12.8 Offline and low-connectivity operation

For field locations with weak internet: lightweight applicant pages, a low-data mode, save-and-continue-later, offline assessment packs, downloadable candidate lists, offline score collection with controlled upload, mobile-friendly forms, and resumption after a connection failure.
